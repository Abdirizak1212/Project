1 Who We Are

Nadaara Hub is an independent digital media and technology platform based in Mogadishu, Somalia. We are the data controller for all personal information collected through our website at nadaarahub.com and associated services including our newsletter and contributor programme.

For all privacy-related enquiries, contact us at privacy@nadaara.com.

 We do not sell your personal data. We never have and we never will. Your data is used solely to operate and improve our platform.

3 How We Use Your Data

We use your data for the following purposes:

  • Account management: Creating and maintaining your account, authentication, and security
  • Service delivery: Displaying personalised content, remembering your preferences, and improving your reading experience
  • Newsletter: Sending you the weekly digest you subscribed to; you can unsubscribe at any time
  • Communication: Responding to your enquiries, support requests, and contributor submissions
  • Analytics: Understanding how readers use our platform so we can improve our content and features
  • Security: Detecting and preventing fraud, spam, and abuse
  • Legal compliance: Meeting our obligations under applicable law

We will never use your data for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects on you without your explicit consent.

4 Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data only when we have a valid legal basis:

  • Contract performance: Processing necessary to provide services you have requested (account registration, newsletter delivery)
  • Legitimate interests: Analytics to improve our platform; security monitoring to protect users
  • Consent: Marketing communications and optional cookies — you can withdraw consent at any time
  • Legal obligation: Where we are required to process data to comply with the law

5 Who We Share Data With

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share data only with trusted service providers who help us operate our platform, and only to the extent necessary:

  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider, which stores your account data on secure servers. Supabase Privacy Policy →
  • Google Fonts & Font Awesome — font and icon delivery (your browser makes requests to their CDNs)
  • GitHub Pages — our hosting provider

We may also disclose your data when required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of our users and the public.

6 Cookies & Tracking

We use minimal, necessary cookies to operate the platform:

  • Session cookies: Keep you logged in during your visit (deleted when you close the browser)
  • Authentication tokens: Stored in your browser’s local storage to maintain your login session across visits (if you select “Remember me”)
  • Rate-limit counters: Stored in local storage to protect login security — no personal data

We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking pixels, or third-party analytics services that profile you. You can clear cookies and local storage at any time through your browser settings, though this will log you out.

7 Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained for as long as your account is active. Deleted within 30 days of account closure.
  • Newsletter subscriptions: Retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion.
  • Usage logs: Aggregated and anonymised after 90 days; individual records deleted.
  • Contact messages: Retained for 12 months, then deleted.
  • Published articles: May remain on the platform after account closure with authorship anonymised, unless you specifically request removal.

8 Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@nadaara.com. We will respond within 15 business days.

Right to Access

Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you

Right to Rectification

Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data

Right to Erasure

Request deletion of your personal data (“right to be forgotten”)

Right to Restriction

Ask us to limit how we process your data in certain situations

Right to Portability

Receive your data in a machine-readable format to transfer elsewhere

Right to Object

Object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing

You also have the right to withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent (e.g. newsletter). Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

9 Data Security

We take the security of your personal data seriously and implement multiple layers of protection:

  • Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed using industry-standard bcrypt algorithms by Supabase
  • HTTPS encryption is enforced on all connections to our platform
  • Brute-force protection: login attempts are rate-limited; accounts are temporarily locked after repeated failures
  • Access control: admin functions are restricted to authorised accounts only; all database access is governed by Row-Level Security policies
  • Session management: authentication tokens expire and are cleared on logout or tab close

Despite these measures, no system is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach that poses a high risk to your rights, we will notify affected users within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach.

10 Children’s Privacy

Nadaara Hub is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately at privacy@nadaara.com and we will delete it promptly.

11 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the “Last Updated” date at the top. For significant changes, we will notify registered users by email at least 14 days before the changes take effect. Continued use of the platform after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
